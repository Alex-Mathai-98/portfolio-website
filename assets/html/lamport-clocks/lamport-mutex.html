The five rules

  1. Request. Timestamp it, send it to everyone, put it in your own queue.
  2. On a request. Put it in your queue and send back a timestamped ack.
  3. Release. Remove your request and send "release" to everyone.
  4. On a release. Remove that process's request from your queue.
  5. Enter when (i) your request is first in your queue under ⇒, and (ii) you have heard something timestamped later than it from every other process.

Channels are FIFO and reliable. Ties on equal timestamps go to P1 ≺ P2 ≺ P3. Clocks follow IR1/IR2: receiving a message with timestamp T sets the clock to max(clock, T) + 1.
